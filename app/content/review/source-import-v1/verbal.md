## gables-1-004

Cryptic most nearly means

- **A.** mysterious
- **B.** basic
- **C.** frightening
- **D.** secure

Answer: A. Cryptic means mysterious.

## gables-1-005

Sommerville is closer to the lake than Derbyville is. Dillon is closer to the lake than Sommerville is. Dillon is closer to the lake than Derbyville is. If the first two statements are true, the third statement is

- **A.** true
- **B.** false
- **C.** uncertain

Answer: A. Dillon is closer than Sommerville, who is closer than Derbyville.

## gables-1-019

Hour is to minute as year is to

- **A.** century
- **B.** clock
- **C.** calendar
- **D.** day

Answer: D. Both pairs relate a time unit to a smaller unit within it.
