# Language — answer guide

## 1. lan-a1-001 — A

A bundle of cables were hanging behind the desk.

Bundle is singular: a bundle was hanging. The other sentences have agreement and complete predicates.

Tip: Check each complete sentence. Choose No error only when all three sentences follow standard written American English.

## 2. lan-a1-002 — A

The results of the experiment supports our prediction.

The plural subject results requires support; experiment is inside an of phrase.

Tip: Check each complete sentence. Choose No error only when all three sentences follow standard written American English.

## 3. lan-a1-003 — B

Either the ushers or the manager have the spare tickets.

With either/or here, the nearer subject manager is singular, so use has.

Tip: Check each complete sentence. Choose No error only when all three sentences follow standard written American English.

## 4. lan-a1-004 — A

The librarian gave Ava and I a tour.

The verb gave needs object pronouns: Ava and me. I is correct in the subject of the other sentence.

Tip: Check each complete sentence. Choose No error only when all three sentences follow standard written American English.

## 5. lan-a1-005 — A

Them are the photographs selected for the exhibit.

A subject demonstrative is needed: Those are the photographs, not Them are.

Tip: Check each complete sentence. Choose No error only when all three sentences follow standard written American English.

## 6. lan-a1-006 — C

By noon, the workers had chose a new route.

Had takes the past participle chosen, not the simple past chose.

Tip: Check each complete sentence. Choose No error only when all three sentences follow standard written American English.

## 7. lan-a1-007 — A
