## gables-1-239

Choose the sentence with an error in punctuation, capitalization, or usage. If there is no error, choose “No mistakes.”

## gables-1-294

Choose the word or words that best join the thoughts together. Doug has run a four-minute mile in his last three races; _____ it is reasonable to conclude that he will run a four-minute mile in the race this weekend.

- **A.** therefore,
- **B.** nevertheless,
- **C.** in contrast,
- **D.** none of these

Answer: A. Therefore introduces the inference from repeated performance; an inference is not a guarantee.
