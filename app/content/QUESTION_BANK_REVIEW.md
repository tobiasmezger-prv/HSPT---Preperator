What number comes next?  7, 12, 17, 22, …

- A. 25
- B. 26
- C. 27
- D. 29

**Key:** C — 27

**Explanation:** Each term increases by 5: 7 + 5 = 12, 12 + 5 = 17, and 17 + 5 = 22. Add 5 again to get 27.

**Quick tip:** Check the gaps between consecutive terms. If they stay the same, add that gap once more.
