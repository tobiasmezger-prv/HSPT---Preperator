# Verbal Skills — answer guide

## 1. ver-a1-003 — B

brief

Concise means expressed in few words. A statement can be precise or blunt while remaining lengthy; incomplete means missing content.

Tip: Decide the word’s meaning before checking whether a synonym or an opposite is requested.

## 2. ver-a1-013 — C

ease

Alleviate means make less severe, not necessarily remove, hide, or delay.

Tip: Decide the word’s meaning before checking whether a synonym or an opposite is requested.

## 3. ver-a1-015 — C

forbid

Prohibit means disallow. Regulation or discouragement may stop short of a ban.

Tip: Decide the word’s meaning before checking whether a synonym or an opposite is requested.

## 4. ver-a1-022 — C

temporary relief

A reprieve is a postponement of punishment or temporary relief; it need not remove blame or give permanent immunity.

Tip: Decide the word’s meaning before checking whether a synonym or an opposite is requested.

## 5. ver-a1-025 — B

confirm

Verify means establish truth or accuracy. Endorsement and repetition do not establish it.

Tip: Decide the word’s meaning before checking whether a synonym or an opposite is requested.

## 6. ver-a1-026 — B

counterfeit

Authentic means genuine; counterfeit means made to pass as genuine. Age, rarity and price are not opposites.

Tip: Decide the word’s meaning before checking whether a synonym or an opposite is requested.

## 7. ver-a1-032 — C

malicious

Benevolent means wishing good; malicious means intending harm. Indifference lacks the opposite positive intention to harm.

Tip: Decide the word’s meaning before checking whether a synonym or an opposite is requested.

## 8. ver-a1-036 — B

descend

Ascend means move upward; descend means move downward. Retreat is backward movement rather than necessarily downward.

Tip: Decide the word’s meaning before checking whether a synonym or an opposite is requested.

## 9. ver-a1-043 — A

active

Dormant means inactive, often temporarily; active is the opposite. Slow development is still activity, but is a narrower and less direct choice.

Tip: Decide the word’s meaning before checking whether a synonym or an opposite is requested.

## 10. ver-a1-046 — C

defiant

Docile means readily guided or taught; defiant actively resists. Independence alone does not imply defiance.

Tip: Decide the word’s meaning before checking whether a synonym or an opposite is requested.

## 11. ver-a1-065 — D

poem

An anecdote is a kind of story; a sonnet is a kind of poem.

Tip: Describe the first relationship in a short sentence and preserve its direction.

## 12. ver-a1-067 — A

mass

A compass indicates direction; a balance scale compares masses.

Tip: Describe the first relationship in a short sentence and preserve its direction.

## 13. ver-a1-068 — A

minute

A telescope helps observe distant objects; a microscope helps observe minute, or very small, objects. Nearness alone is not the defining contrast.

Tip: State the relationship, then keep the same direction and degree of specificity.

## 14. ver-a1-069 — B

ending

A preface appears at the beginning of a book; an epilogue appears at its end.

Tip: Describe the first relationship in a short sentence and preserve its direction.

## 15. ver-a1-075 — D

conjecture

Conjecture is a proposed explanation without established proof; the others support or establish a claim.

Tip: Identify the precise shared relationship; do not choose only by how familiar a word feels.

## 16. ver-a1-078 — B

implication

An implication conveys something indirectly; the others name explicit statements.

Tip: Identify the precise shared relationship; do not choose only by how familiar a word feels.

## 17. ver-a1-082 — A

appendix

An appendix is supplementary material; the others name divisions within the main text. The question concerns structural role, not physical attachment.

Tip: Identify the precise shared relationship; do not choose only by how familiar a word feels.

## 18. ver-a1-089 — D

Blue.

Not striped rules out green; not red rules out red. The exhaustive third option is blue.

Tip: Use only the stated conditions. Check every candidate against all of them.

## 19. ver-a1-094 — D

Q.

Turning Q on forces R on and would create three lit lamps with P, contradicting exactly two. Either R or S can be the second lamp.

Tip: Use only the stated conditions. Check every candidate against all of them.

## 20. ver-a1-100 — C

At least one stated fact about the display is incorrect.

The stated rules allow six or eight hooks, never seven. They do not identify which statement fails or establish a specific defect.

Tip: Use only the stated conditions. Check every candidate against all of them.
